# Toddler Coloring Book

A reference site on first coloring books for children ages 1 to 3:
[toddlercoloringbook.com](https://www.toddlercoloringbook.com)

The site helps parents, childcare staff and specialists choose a coloring
book that matches what a child can do right now, rather than the age
printed on the cover. It covers the four stages of first drawing, what a
page should look like at each stage, what is normal at the table at one,
two and three years old, and what an age label on a cover does and does
not mean.

It carries a free picker that names the stage a child is at now, pages by
age, guide articles, ten pages free to print, and the questions parents
ask, answered in full. Nothing on the site sits behind a form, a login or
a paywall.

The site is published by Magic of Discoveries LLC, an independent family
publisher in Miami, Florida. The publisher's own book, First Coloring Book
for Toddlers Ages 1-3, is used on the site as a worked example: 111 large
drawings with thick outlines, one per page, printed on one side. It is
published in English and Spanish in print, and in Russian as a file to
print at home.

Three languages, twenty-eight pages each. Built with Next.js, deployed on
Vercel.
